## What this domain covers

Engineering for the coordination of customer engagements across projects, teams, and stakeholders. Repeatable patterns for intake, scope, hand-off, status, and outcome reporting that hold under engagement growth.

**What it isn't.** A single-system point solution. A regulated-industry deliverable. A founder's zero-to-one build.
